How the model was made

A little more than meets the blueprint.

We turned the original 2D plan into an interactive 3D view, keeping the documented layout and illustrating details where the drawings leave room for interpretation.

Taken from the planRoom arrangement, corridors, partitions, door positions, room names, area figures, workstation counts (WS) and the lobby and store-room sizes, traced from the supplied drawing.
InferredDesk and chair arrangement inside each room, furniture forms, planters, sanitary fixtures and reception furniture. These follow the drawing's symbols but are illustrative.
Not providedWall and ceiling heights, true dimensions, windows and structural details are not in the drawing. Model units are drawing units and heights are illustrative only.
Figures shownAreas are shown as printed on the plan (the plan marks them in square metres). The two supplied renderings differ slightly in a few labels; the model follows the layout plan.
